Welcome to Applegate Dance Studio! We offer a variety of dance classes for all ages and skill levels. Join us to learn, grow, and have fun through the art of dance.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide a nurturing and inspiring environment where dancers can develop their skills, express themselves creatively, and build confidence through the art of dance.

Vision Statement

Our vision is to be a leading dance studio in the community, known for our commitment to excellence, innovation in dance education, and fostering a love for dance in every student.

Values Statement

We value integrity, respect, creativity, and dedication. We strive to create a positive and inclusive atmosphere where every dancer feels valued and supported in their journey.
